# Real county pre-pilot decision gate

Owner-approved amendment, 2026-10-16: Phase 1 develops a fictional county environment
using synthetic information, with English and Kiswahili support. Real county selection,
participants, catalogue approval, staffing and operational budget are deferred to
this gate. They no longer block synthetic foundation work. No real pilot, personal
records, county credentials, confidential documents, payments, public deployment or
live county integration is authorized by the development milestone.

The modular-monolith design and future phase boundaries remain unchanged. Phase 2
identity/conversation implementation, Phase 3 model calls, later RAG/tools, and AWS
deployment each require separate approval. This gate does not permit a live model
call: account/model, data categories, privacy basis and spend approval are still
required before any future external inference, even with synthetic inputs.

## Required decisions before a real county pilot

| Decision / evidence | Accountable party | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| County, service scope, participants and invitation/access process | Repository owner and designated county service owner | Deferred; no real county approved |
| Official service catalogue, source provenance, review schedule, escalation contacts | County departmental content owners | Deferred; synthetic examples convey no official policy |
| English/Kiswahili content and independent evaluation review | Designated bilingual reviewers | Reviewers not yet designated |
| Staffing, support, incident response and operational ownership | Repository owner and county operations owner | Deferred |
| Operational/model/hosting budget and spending limits | Funding owner | Deferred; no paid operations authorized here |
| Controller/processor mapping, legal basis, notice, rights handling, DPIA/registration assessment | Designated county privacy/legal lead | Deferred; no compliance approval claimed |
| Retention/deletion, audit access and backup/restore obligations | Privacy lead and operations owner | Phase 0 values remain proposals requiring approval |
| Hosting/data locations, provider/subprocessors and overseas transfer safeguards | Privacy/security lead and owner | Deferred; AWS/region not selected |
| Security/accessibility/quality evidence, residual-risk acceptance and launch decision | Owner with relevant county reviewers | Not assessed for a real pilot |

These are role responsibilities, not claims that a county partner or reviewer exists.
No checkbox or successful foundation test substitutes for the required decisions.
Record named reviewers, dated decisions and supporting evidence before this gate can
pass. Do not put personal data or confidential agreements in public issues or commits.

## Gate completion

The repository owner must explicitly approve the actual pilot scope and evidence
after privacy/security/content/operations review. A later reviewed PR updates this
record with non-sensitive decision references and remaining conditions. A merge,
synthetic demo or green CI never automatically authorizes a real-data pilot, deployment,
payment integration, county-system access, or another phase.
